05 / Technical Interfaces

High-Speed & Low-Speed Communication

Core interfaces and protocols in modern power electronics systems.

🚀 High-Speed Interfaces

  • PCIe (PCI Express) - Multi-lane configurations
  • USB 3.0/3.1/3.2 - High-speed data transfer
  • LVDS (Low Voltage Differential Signaling)
  • MIPI CSI/DSI - Mobile industry protocols
  • Ethernet (1GbE, 10GbE) - Network communication
  • Differential signaling with impedance matching

🔗 Low-Speed Interfaces

  • I²C (Inter-Integrated Circuit) - 100kHz to 400kHz
  • SPI (Serial Peripheral Interface) - Synchronous communication
  • UART/RS-232/RS-485 - Serial protocols
  • CAN Bus - Automotive communication
  • GPIO (General Purpose Input/Output)
  • Modbus - Industrial protocol

⚡ Power & Signal Integrity

  • PDN (Power Distribution Network) design
  • Impedance control (50Ω, 75Ω, 100Ω differential)
  • Via stitching and layer stackup optimization
  • EMI/EMC suppression techniques
  • Ground plane strategies
  • Crosstalk mitigation and signal routing

🔋 Power Management

  • Multi-rail power delivery systems
  • DC-DC converters and buck/boost topologies
  • Battery management systems (BMS)
  • Thermal design and heat dissipation
  • Inrush current management
  • Protection circuits (OVP, UVP, OCP)

Major Projects & Achievements

5G Radio Unit (N78)
2022 – 2023
Telecom & 5G Infrastructure

Project Overview

Engineered a 5G radio unit operating on the N78 frequency band featuring 8 transmitters and 8 receivers based on TDD (Time Division Duplex) methodology. This project encompassed complete design verification and compliance certification.

Technical Achievements

  • Designed multi-layer PCB with high-speed RF traces and impedance control
  • Optimized power distribution for 8×8 MIMO architecture
  • Implemented advanced thermal management for continuous operation
  • DVT (Design Verification Testing) — validated all functional and RF specifications
  • Compliance certification preparation — met regulatory standards
  • Design support throughout manufacturing and production phases
Altium Designer RF Design MIMO N78 Band TDD DVT

Key Learning

"Mastering RF design taught me that precision in PCB layout is as critical as circuit theory. Every trace, via, and ground connection influences system performance."

V2L Inverter System

Vehicle-to-Load & Power Conversion

Developed a Vehicle-to-Load (V2L) inverter enabling vehicles to supply power to external devices. This bidirectional charging solution bridges vehicle and external load ecosystems using advanced power conversion.

Technical Achievements

  • PCB design review and layout optimization for manufacturing
  • PRD (Product Requirements Document) development and validation

11.5kW & 7.2kW Wall Mount EVSE
May 2024 – July 2025
IEC standard based type 2 EVSE

Project Overview

Designed and led production transition for an 11.5kW wall-mounted EVSE charger. As the primary hardware designer, I orchestrated the entire design-to-production journey including Designing , compliance and manufacturing.

Design Responsibilities

  • Schematic Design: Complete power stage, control circuits, and protection mechanisms in OrCAD
  • PCB Layout: Multi-layer design with emphasis on thermal dissipation and high-current routing
  • Design Review: Comprehensive review for manufacturability and compliance
  • Documentation: PRD, HDD, DVT reports, and EMI/EMC compliance certification
  • Production Leadership: Led design from ideation through full production ramp
  • Compliance: Ensured adherence to IEC 61851, EMI & EMC Standards and regional EVSE standards
11.5kW EVSE Mass Production IEC 61851 EMI/EMC Hardware Design

Automatic Grid Disconnect System
Aug 2026 → Future
Next-Generation Grid Management || UL Certified

Project Vision

Developing a UL-standard-compliant Advanced Grid-Distributed (AGD) energy system that integrates the utility grid, solar generation, and a bi-directional 11.5 kW EVSE for V2G/V2H applications. The system is designed with islanding capability to provide safe and reliable backup power during grid outages, enabling intelligent energy management and bidirectional power flow between the grid, solar, EV, and home loads while meeting required safety and regulatory requirements

Core Objectives

  • V2G (Vehicle-to-Grid): Enable EVs to feed power back to the grid during peak demand
  • G2V (Grid-to-Vehicle): Intelligent vehicle charging optimized for grid stability
  • S2G (Solar-to-Grid): Direct solar array integration for distributed renewable energy
  • Power Management: Multi-directional power flow with 8+ transmitters and 8+ receivers for maximum flexibility
  • Protection & Intelligence: Implement safe islanding functionality to maintain critical home loads during grid outages while ensuring proper grid isolation and protection.
  • Optimize renewable energy utilization by intelligently managing solar generation, EV charging/discharging, and grid power consumption.
V2G G2V S2G Grid Integration Renewable Energy Future Vision

The Big Picture

"The AGD represents my ultimate vision—a device that bridges personal transportation, renewable energy, and grid infrastructure. It's the convergence of everything I've learned in power electronics, scaled for global impact on sustainable energy."
